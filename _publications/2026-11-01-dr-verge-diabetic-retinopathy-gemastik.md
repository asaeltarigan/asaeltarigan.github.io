Gemastik student team supervised by Gabriel Asael Tarigan (weekly consultations, clinical problem-scoping, and clinician/dataset liaison; name not listed on the manuscript for administrative reasons). DR-VERGE is a five-level ordinal diabetic retinopathy grading framework on dual-view fundus images, combining CORAL ordinal modelling, lightweight Complementarity-Shift Distillation (CSD), and INT8 quantization. The deployment-optimized student (328,588 parameters) is roughly 162x smaller and 55x faster than the teacher while retaining 98.96% of internal QWK, targeting diabetic retinopathy screening on resource-limited CPU environments such as health facilities in Indonesia's 3T regions.
