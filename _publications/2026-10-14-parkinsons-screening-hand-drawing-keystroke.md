A multimodal AI-based Parkinson's disease screening system combining hand-drawing analysis (spiral and wave tests analyzed with EfficientNet-B0 and handcrafted geometric features) and keystroke dynamics (Tappy dataset via XGBoost), integrated with a weighted late-fusion strategy.
