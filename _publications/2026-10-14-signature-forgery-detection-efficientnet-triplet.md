A writer-independent signature verification system that freezes a pretrained EfficientNet-B0 backbone and fine-tunes only its embedding head with batch-hard triplet loss, mapping signatures to L2-normalized embeddings compared by cosine similarity and evaluated on writers held out entirely from training.
