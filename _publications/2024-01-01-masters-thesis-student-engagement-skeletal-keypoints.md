Master's thesis in Computer Science, BINUS University, Jakarta, 2024. The work asks whether classroom engagement can be recognised from skeletal keypoints alone, without the cost of full-body pose stacks or per-frame manual annotation: a lightweight pipeline built on YOLOv8m person detection and MediaPipe keypoint estimation, evaluated against the YOLOv4 and OpenPose baseline on accuracy and inference speed.

This thesis is the origin of the 2025 journal article of the same title in the IAENG International Journal of Computer Science; the article is the peer-reviewed version, this record is the underlying thesis. Code, models and companion data are in the [skeletal-keypoint-engagement repository](https://github.com/asaeltarigan/skeletal-keypoint-engagement).
