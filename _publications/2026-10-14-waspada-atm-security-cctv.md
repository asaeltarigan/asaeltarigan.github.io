Automated Teller Machine (ATM) areas remain frequent targets of violent crime, yet conventional CCTV serves mainly as passive evidence storage rather than as an active prevention tool. This study proposes WASPADA (Weapon and Suspicious Pose Alert & Detection Aggregator), a real-time surveillance framework that transforms standard ATM CCTV into an early-warning system. The framework combines two YOLO26-based modules: a weapon and face-attribute detector, and a suspicious-pose module utilizing COCO pre-trained YOLO26n-Pose weights. Outputs are aggregated through a decision-level threat-scoring mechanism, where physical and behavioral indicators contribute calibrated weights toward one of four security levels. A maximum-threat classification automatically dispatches a Telegram alert containing the annotated frame. On a held-out test set, both object detectors achieved a precision of at least 0.900 and mAP@0.5 over 0.720, maintaining an average inference time of 8.8ms per frame. Live testing over 826 logged events confirms the scoring mechanism effectively escalates through all levels, reserving critical alerts for genuine weapons or face-concealment presence. These results indicate that decision-level fusion of object detection and pose estimation is a practical path toward active, low-cost ATM security monitoring.
