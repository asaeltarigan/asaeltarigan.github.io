Final-year BINUS student capstone, supervised by Gabriel Asael Tarigan. Seeing Through Sound is a real-time object-detection system that conveys the position of objects to visually impaired users through audio feedback, built on YOLO26n trained on a unified 22-class urban-environment dataset combining five public Roboflow datasets.
