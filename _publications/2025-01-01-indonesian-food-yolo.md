The application of deep learning for automated dietary assessment of Indonesian cuisine is severely hampered by the critical lack of large-scale, publicly available image datasets, which limits model performance. This study aims to overcome these data limitations by developing a high-performance, integrated system for accurate food recognition and automated nutritional analysis. Our primary contribution is a novel end-to-end framework that uniquely combines a fine-tuned object detection model with a generative language model to provide practical, user-centric dietary feedback. For the methodology, we evaluated multiple YOLO architectures on the CalorEase dataset, selecting YOLOv9s as the optimal model. After extended training, it achieved a mean average precision (mAP@0.5) of 95.9%. The system integrates these detection results with a nutritional database for calorie calculation and employs the Gemma 3 language model to generate a concise summary and dietary suggestions in Bahasa Indonesia. This study demonstrates a robust solution for automated dietary analysis, establishing a strong benchmark for Indonesian food recognition.
