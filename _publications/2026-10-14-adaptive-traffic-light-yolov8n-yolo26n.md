Final-year BINUS student capstone, supervised by Gabriel Asael Tarigan. A comparative evaluation of YOLOv8n and YOLO26n with ByteTrack for real-time vehicle detection in adaptive traffic management systems, benchmarked on MAE, MAPE, FPS, and inference latency across varied traffic and lighting conditions in Jakarta.
