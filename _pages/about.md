I am a lecturer in Artificial Intelligence at [BINUS University](https://www.binus.ac.id) (School of Computer Science), where I designed and authored the AI program's core curriculum and build the production systems that run it: Kaggle-style competition platforms, course quiz portals, and interactive teaching tools.

My research interests are deep learning for time-series forecasting (hybrid LSTM-GRU architectures and their parallelization), the engineering of applied ML systems, and educational technology. I am currently preparing a doctoral application for salaried PhD positions in Europe (Germany-first).

**What I do: I teach it, I build it, I run it.**

## Research interests

- Deep Learning
- Time-Series Forecasting
- ML Systems & MLOps
- Applied NLP
- Educational Technology

## Selected projects

- **ML Competition Platform (ARISE)**: a Kaggle-style competition platform for BINUS students, self-hosted on a single $5 VPS (FastAPI, cloudflared, daily submission caps, admin analytics).
- **Course Quiz Portals**: weekly quiz portals for ML courses: single-attempt, NIM-gated submissions with instant feedback and live results.
- **Backpropagation Teaching Tool**: a browser-based tool that teaches backpropagation through a 4-beat story: *guess, measure, blame, nudge*.
- **Build Diary**: an [automated public log](https://github.com/asaeltarigan/build-diary) of what I ship, committed truthfully week by week.
