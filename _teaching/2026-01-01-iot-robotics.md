A 2+2 credit course (theory and practicum) introducing the integration of IoT, robotics, and AI: how sensors, embedded systems, and communication protocols collect, transmit, and process data, from basic IoT concepts to designing intelligent systems that integrate devices, cloud platforms, and decision-making logic. Students build an end-to-end IoT ecosystem from prototype to deployment.

## Sessions

- **Sessions 1-3: Embedded Foundations** — introduction to IoT, embedded systems and ESP32, embedded programming fundamentals, digital electronics
- **Sessions 4-5: Sensing and Actuation** — sensors and data acquisition, actuators and embedded control
- **Sessions 6-8: Connectivity** — networking and Wi-Fi communication, wireless communication (Bluetooth, BLE, ESP-NOW), cloud connectivity and IoT platforms (HTTP and MQTT)
- **Sessions 9-10: Intelligent IoT** — IoT visualization and intelligent automation, edge AI and TinyML
- **Sessions 11-13: Systems and Deployment** — cloud AI and intelligent IoT systems, designing intelligent IoT systems, final presentation

## Learning Outcomes

- Explain IoT architectures, embedded systems, and robotics fundamentals
- Implement IoT systems using sensors, microcontrollers, and communication protocols
- Apply data-driven and AI techniques in IoT and robotic systems
- Evaluate complete IoT ecosystems for real-world applications
