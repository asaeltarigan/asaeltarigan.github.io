A single-file, browser-based teaching tool that makes backpropagation intuitive.

- Teaches the algorithm through a 4-beat story: **guess** (forward pass), **measure** (loss), **blame** (chain rule), **nudge** (update)
- XOR as the hero example: the classic problem a single neuron cannot solve
- Used in my deep learning classes to replace abstract derivations with something students can click and see
